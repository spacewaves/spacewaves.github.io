How to play Space Waves

Your wave travels forward on its own at a steady pace. You only decide whether it climbs or dives. Stay inside the tunnel, slip past the magenta blocks, and see how many metres you can cover before the walls close in.

Controls

  • Computer: hold Space, W, ↑ or the left mouse button to climb. Let go to dive. Press P to pause.
  • Phone or tablet: press and hold anywhere on the game area to climb, lift your finger to dive.

Difficulty modes

  • Easy: slower speed and a roomy tunnel. Best for learning how sharply the wave turns.
  • Normal: balanced speed with a tunnel that tightens in the first few seconds.
  • Hard: fast, narrow and unforgiving, built for players chasing leaderboard-level distances.

In every mode the corridor gradually narrows until it reaches a minimum width, so the longer you last, the more precise you must be.

Tips for a longer run

  • Tap in a rhythm. Short, even taps keep the wave near the middle of the tunnel and give you room to correct.
  • Look ahead of the wave. Watch the next block, not the arrow. By the time a wall is under your nose it is too late to turn.
  • Commit to the long slopes. On a steady incline, hold the button instead of stuttering. Constant input is smoother than nervous tapping.
  • Pick the open half. Each magenta block fills the top or bottom half of the tunnel. Decide early which side you will fly through.
  • Practice on Easy first. Learning the turning rate on a wide tunnel makes Normal and Hard feel much fairer.

About this wave runner

Wave runners are a small arcade genre built on a single idea: your craft always moves at a 45-degree angle, either up or down. There is no shooting, no power-ups and no inventory. This browser edition keeps that purity and adds a procedurally built tunnel, so no two runs share the same layout.

Because each run is generated on the fly, results come down to timing and nerve. Your best distance is saved locally in your browser, so you can come back and try to beat it.

Frequently asked questions

Is Space Waves free to play?

Yes. It runs in your browser and costs nothing. There is nothing to download or install.

Which keys control the wave?

Hold Space, W, the Up arrow or the mouse button to climb. Release to dive. On touch screens, press and hold anywhere on the game.

Can I play Space Waves on my phone?

Yes. The game scales to any screen and uses simple press-and-hold touch input.

Is my best score saved?

Your best distance is stored in your own browser, so it stays on your device and is cleared if you wipe site data.

Which difficulty should I start with?

Start on Easy. The tunnel is wide and slow, which lets you learn how quickly the wave turns.
